# WEEK 6

### **What We Did This Week:**

1. **Realized the need for an intial "Enforced Acquisition" Process**
- Goal is to ensure that the user's camera view meets certain minimum criteria prior to image capture
- Saves preprocessing efforts later on

2. **Detailing the above process (4 metrics initially):**
- Presence of bill in image
- Spatial Coverage of Image
- Minimum clarity of image (measuring blur)
- Range of Illumination of image (brightness)

3. **Tried testing them using a live feed from Phone with OpenCV**
